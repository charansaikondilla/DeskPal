# Image generation prompts

Built-in image tool; primary reference: `lord ganesh .png`. These prompts generated concept boards only. Production directions and acceptance criteria are in ANIMATION_DESIGN.md.

## Personality board

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: premium animation art-direction contact sheet for DeskPal. This is a twelve-pose DESIGN GUIDE, not a finished animation atlas.
Input image 1: character identity reference. Match the exact cute baby Ganesh of the reference: peach-tan elephant skin, pink inner ears, dark brown dense curls, detailed gold crown with central red stone, peacock feather, small red tilak, yellow dhoti with red/gold border, bracelets, necklaces, sweet chibi proportions. Exactly two arms. Retain this identity and soft polished 3D rendering throughout.
Background: entirely opaque pure white #FFFFFF, no transparency, no checkerboard, no black, no scene, no floor shadows. Clean spacious four-column three-row editorial contact sheet. All figures fully inside cells, generous equal white gutters, no grid lines. Large high resolution landscape image. Consistent studio lighting and character scale.
Twelve separate hero poses, left-to-right row-by-row:
1 standing relaxed with both feet visible, eyes open, a small welcoming smile.
2 seated cross-legged with palms on knees, calm breath, both eyes gently closed.
3 same seated meditation, one eye closed and other eye mischievously peeking open, tiny smile.
4 joyful jump reaching upward toward ONE airborne cream-gold teardrop modak (pointed pleated steamed dumpling, NOT a round laddu).
5 happy mouth catch of that single modak falling downward, head tipped back, hands open, no extra sweets.
6 satisfied pose after swallowing, hand gently patting round tummy, no transparent anatomy, no sweet visible inside body.
7 playful peek: only Ganesh head and one gripping hand emerging from behind a slim neutral vertical window-edge card. This card is only in this cell and is a conceptual window edge.
8 cute thirsty seated slump leaning gently sideways with drooping ears, mild tiredness; no distress or injury.
9 drinking from a small simple blue water cup, brightening eyes, trunk curled naturally aside.
10 serene blessing with raised right palm facing viewer, relaxed other hand, calm eyes, subtle golden star accents.
11 seated studying an open blank book on lap, focused kind expression, one finger tracking the page; no lettering on book.
12 Ganesh riding his small friendly soft-gray mouse companion, side three-quarter view facing right, mouse running cheerfully, both entire figures visible; mouse large enough to support him in this playful stylized depiction.
No titles, no captions, no typography, no watermarks. No additional figures in any cell except the mouse in cell 12. Preserve crown and trunk anatomy. Clear readable silhouettes. No violent collapse, no human-body anatomy overlays. Premium inviting animation concept art.
```

## Mouse play and happy jump board

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: DeskPal animation storyboard continuation, 8 key poses in a spacious 4-column 2-row white-background contact sheet; not a finished frame atlas.
Input 1 is the character identity reference. Match the same baby Ganesh face, dark curly hair, gold crown with red stone, peacock feather, pink ears, short trunk, gold bracelets, warm peach skin and yellow dhoti. Exactly two arms, gentle glossy 3D style.
Pure opaque white background, no transparency, no grid borders, no shadows on the ground, no typography. Every pose fully visible within its cell, consistent character proportions and camera distance, generous white gutters. Premium art direction; cheerful, respectful.
Row 1 depicts playful mouse interaction:
cell 1 Ganesh kneels, one open hand extended to a small friendly soft-gray mouse, mouse sniffing his fingertips.
cell 2 both running playfully to the right, Ganesh leaning forward, mouse trotting ahead; show both full bodies and mouse tail.
cell 3 Ganesh sitting on the ground catching the mouse gently in cupped hands, mouse relaxed; absolutely no grabbing its tail, no violence.
cell 4 mouse perched happily on Ganesh knee, Ganesh laughing and petting it gently.
Row 2 happy jump key poses:
cell 5 standing anticipation with knees bent and hands down.
cell 6 pushing up from both feet, arms swinging upward.
cell 7 airborne joyful jump, both hands up, both feet clearly in air, delighted eyes.
cell 8 soft landing squat, knees bent, delighted smile, arms balancing.
No food props in this sheet. Same mouse design across row 1. No duplication of mouse within a cell. Avoid extra fingers, extra limbs, clipped feathers, inconsistent clothing.
```
